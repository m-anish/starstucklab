# Handheld Weather Station — Gust & Gloom

A pocket weather station that is polite about humidity and extremely suspicious of umbrellas.

## Description

Gust & Gloom is a compact, handheld weather station that fits in your pocket but provides professional-grade meteorological data. This elegantly designed device combines multiple sensors with a beautiful OLED display and companion mobile app to deliver comprehensive weather information wherever you go.

Perfect for:
- Hikers and outdoor enthusiasts
- Farmers and gardeners
- Meteorologists and weather enthusiasts
- Educators and students
- Emergency preparedness

## Key Features

### Sensors
- **Temperature**: -40°C to 85°C with 0.1°C resolution
- **Humidity**: 0-100% RH with 0.1% resolution
- **Barometric Pressure**: 300-1100 hPa with 0.1 hPa resolution
- **Wind Speed**: 0-50 m/s with 0.1 m/s resolution
- **Wind Direction**: 360° with 1° resolution
- **Rain Gauge**: 0-999 mm with 0.2 mm resolution

### Display & Interface
- **OLED Screen**: 128x64 pixel display with clear readings
- **Touch Controls**: Intuitive capacitive buttons
- **Backlight**: Automatic or manual control
- **Units**: Switch between metric and imperial
- **Languages**: Multiple language support

### Data & Connectivity
- **Internal Storage**: 30 days of 1-minute interval data
- **Bluetooth**: Real-time data streaming to mobile devices
- **USB**: Data export and firmware updates
- **Battery**: 1000mAh LiPo with 30+ days runtime
