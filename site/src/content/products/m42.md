## Description

The M42 Dobsonian is a compact, ultra-light telescope designed for backyard astronomy and occasional philosophical contemplation. Built with precision 3D-printed components and high-quality optics, it offers excellent light-gathering capability in a surprisingly portable package.

Perfect for:

* Urban stargazing with limited space
* Travel to dark sky locations
* Educational demonstrations
* Casual astronomical exploration

## What's Included

* 3D-printed customizable optical tube assembly with non-rotating helical focuser
* 3D-printed dobsonian mount with altitude bearings and Teflon pads
* 25mm/52°FOV Plössl & 9mm/68°FOV Plössl eyepieces
* Red-dot finder
* Collimation cheshire
* Mount tensioner to control friction
* Assembly instructions

## Build Notes

This telescope represents the perfect balance between optical performance and mechanical simplicity. The 3D-printed components ensure consistent quality while allowing for customization. The helical focuser provides smooth, precise focusing without rotation, making it ideal for both visual and photographic use.

## Assembly

Assembly takes approximately 30-45 minutes and requires only basic tools. The modular design allows for easy transport and storage.

## Care & Maintenance

* Store in a dry, dust-free environment
* Clean optics with proper astronomical cleaning supplies
* Periodically check and adjust collimation
* Lubricate bearings as needed
